             o   The agent or policyholder must remit the gross premium and TFPA will pay

             o   Policyholder must agree to allow inspection of the property at the
                 Associations request.

Binding Authority
   A. The agent shall have no binding authority to bind new business, renewal or increase the
      exposure of the TFPA on existing policies.

   B. New policies and increases in dwelling values will be considered bound only upon the issuance
      of a policy or, if an increase in dwelling values, upon issuance of an amended declarations.

   C. In the event a Hurricane is within the area of 80 degrees West Longitude and 20 degrees North
      Latitude, the TFPA may suspend the taking of applications in the state, when Texas Windstorm
      Insurance Association (TWIA) suspends accepting applications. The TFPA may also suspend the
      taking of applications when and in the part of the state it finds that an ongoing event threatens
      to create an imminent danger of catastrophic losses.

This suspension shall remain in force until the general manager has determined that the hurricane or
event is no longer a threat to property located in Texas.

Important Note: There shall be no backdating of coverage.
